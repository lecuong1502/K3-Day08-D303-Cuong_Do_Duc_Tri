1. What is National Medical Insurance (Bảo hiểm Y tế Học sinh-Sinh viên)?

xxxxxx

National Medical Insurance (NMI) for Vietnamese students is a compulsory medical insurance
scheme established by Decree 105/2014/ND-CP, effective from January 1, 2015.

Universities are required to collect this fee from Vietnamese students and then transfer it to
the Social Insurance Unit of  the city where the campus is located.

2. Why is National Medical Insurance compulsory?

If  you are a Vietnamese student, joining the National Medical Insurance is not just a choice — it
is required by law (Decree 105/2014/NĐ-CP, effective from January 1, 2015). This policy helps
make sure you are covered for medical care when you need it, and it applies to all students, even
if  you are taking a Leave of  absence or on an exchange program.

Find out more information at https://baohiemxahoi.gov.vn.

3. How to register for National Medical Insurance?

To register NMI, students select Hospital and provide Social Insurance Number (Mã số Bảo hiểm
Xã hội)

Step 1: Select Hospital

Students complete Step 6 on Pre-enrolment Checklist on myRMIT

1.Select No for the question ‘I have purchased my own Medical Insurance’.

2.Insert the National ID card number in section 1 and select the preferred hospital or clinic

in section 2.

3.Click Confirm to submit the registration form.

4. Am I eligible for National Medical Insurance waiver?

You are eligible to apply for the NMI waiver if you have a valid NMI card (it must be an NMI card
as shown above; other private insurance cards are NOT accepted). Please complete the following
steps to apply for the NMI waiver.

Step 1: Select Yes for the question ‘I have purchased my own Medical Insurance’ and click
Confirm to proceed to the NMI Waiver Form.

Step 2: Select Yes for the question ‘I have read and consented to the above statements’.

Step 3: Click Add Attachment to upload a photo of your valid NMI.

Step 4: Click Submit to submit the Waiver Form.

Please contact Student Connect if you need support.

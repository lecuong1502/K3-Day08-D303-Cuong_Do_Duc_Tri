ACCOMMODATIONS
COST OF LIVING
Student could arrive 4 weeks prior to university’s start date to have
time to arrange long-term accommodation.
Estimated expense is approximately from $600 - 800 per month,
including bills:
Internet: $15
Food: $100-200
Accommodation: $350-550
Phone + 3G: $14-20
Electricity, gas, water: $50
Transportation: 40-50 cents/km (for taxi)

& HCMC
Living off campus enables you to immerse in the dynamic
cosmopolitan life of Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh city. In the
surrounding areas of RMIT Vietnam campuses, you will find a
range of apartment complexes that offer a conducive
environment, while also providing convenient access to
university support.
Some accommodation recommendations could be found on our
pre-arrival guide or RMIT website.
For more information on recommended accommodations, please

INTERNATIONAL
STUDENTS
SUPPORT
SERVICES
Airport pick-up service
Free for newbies. Students will receive an email to register the service
prior to the semester.
Buddy team
A Buddy is a current student well-equipped with the skills and
knowledge to help students adapt to RMIT and living in Vietnam,
including:
Living tips in Vietnam
Accessing services that can aid students living at RMIT Vietnam
Housing and banking supports
Developing self-management and self-awareness skills
Free Language and Culture Exchange Workshop
Vietnamese buddies will share their stories about the Vietnamese
language and culture, while students can share stories about their
country and culture.
Community Engagement/ Charity trips
Every semester, international students and their buddies organise a
community engagement trip or a charity trip together to disadvantaged
areas of Vietnam. Activities often include making handmade gifts and
organizing events to generate donations.

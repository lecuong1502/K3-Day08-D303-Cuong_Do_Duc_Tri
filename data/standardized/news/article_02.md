We are thrilled to announce R:Loop - From waste to value, an amazing event that promises a journey filled with joy and benefits! 
This is a fantastic opportunity to gain practical sustainability skills, earn meaningful rewards, exchange recyclable items and enhance library literacy, navigate the RMIT Vietnam Library digital resources, physical spaces and specialised services via gamified challenges. 
![two cartoon animals reading, surrounded by eco-themed icons and highlights sustainability.](https://www.rmit.edu.vn/assets/vn/en/assets-for-production/images/libraryvn-site/events/r-loop/rloop-main-poster.jpg)
## Event details
Join us from **July 6 – 16** to explore new knowledge, make new friends and create unforgettable experiences.
This event is opened for all RMIT students and staff (both SGS and HN campuses)
![two cartoon animals reading, surrounded by eco-themed icons and highlights sustainability.](https://www.rmit.edu.vn/assets/vn/en/assets-for-production/images/libraryvn-site/events/r-loop/rloop-event-detail.jpg)
### Promotion booth
**6-10 July; 10 AM - 2 PM**
**Booth SGS:** Library entry door and 2A, 2B, in front of Soya house   
**Booth HN:** Library entry door (1.2.006)
#1 Scavenger Hunt   
#2 Karuta (Double points for World Questival series   
#3 Matching Pairs (Double points for World Questival series)   
#4 Otoshi (Double points for World Questival series)
![two cartoon animals reading, surrounded by eco-themed icons and highlights sustainability.](https://www.rmit.edu.vn/assets/vn/en/assets-for-production/images/libraryvn-site/events/r-loop/rloop-main-event.jpg)
### R-Loop festival
Decode the library’s best spots and survival tools through exciting offline games.
Swap your recyclables for exclusive, eco-friendly lifestyle items and trendy study essentials.
Level up your green knowledge through fun mini-games and hands-on cultural workshops.
Discover how your small, daily choices can spark a major wave of positive change for our planet.
Master the traditional Japanese art of Furoshiki wrapping and explore unique waste exhibitions.
![two cartoon animals reading, surrounded by eco-themed icons and highlights sustainability.](https://www.rmit.edu.vn/assets/vn/en/assets-for-production/images/libraryvn-site/events/r-loop/rloop-prizes.jpg)

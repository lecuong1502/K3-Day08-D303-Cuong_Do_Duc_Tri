## Beyond the pages
Step right up for an adventure at the “Beyond the Pages: Critical and Sustainable Thinking”. This event is packed with quirky games, creative challenges designed to make you laugh while you learn. Team up with new friends, put your thinking caps on and discover how sustainable thinking can be both clever and cool. 
Come along for good vibes, big ideas and plenty of fun! You might just leave as a critical thinking champion!

Immersive Experience: Stories Take Shape
## Why join our event?
  * Gain practical techniques to analyse and evaluate information more effectively
  * Discover how to apply sustainable practices and thinking to real-world problems
  * Improve your ability to tackle assignments, participate in discussions
  * Meet fellow students and workshop leaders who share interest in critical and sustainable ideas, building valuable networks for future collaboration.
